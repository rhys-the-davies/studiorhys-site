Simplifier, writer, builder.

I am a facilitator. I love to help experts work together effectively. I'm experienced enough in technology, design, and sustainability to understand technicalities and translate complexity into simple, accessible roadmaps we build together.

I hold a master's degree in mechatronic engineering, but I'm not an engineer. I write stories every week but I'm not an author. I've helped raise millions of euros but I'm not a founder. I love working with specialists, facilitating making their work simple, and supporting them to make their missions a reality.

Along the way I've worked with open source tech companies, sustainability consultancies, social enterprises, climate researchers, traceability experts, and many more. Everywhere I see organisations with great teams, and inspiring missions, but missing the generalist tech or operational experience that isn't core to their business but would bring far more value.
