Who I am

Studio Rhys is the sole proprietorship of Rhys Davies, based in Amsterdam (KVK 97713430). For anything about your data, email rhys@studio-rhys.com.

What I collect, and why

  • When you get in touch: your name, contact details and whatever you tell me, so I can reply. If we work together, I also use them to run the project and send invoices.

Who else handles it

How long I keep it

Messages that don't lead to work are deleted within two years. Client records and invoices are kept for seven years, as Dutch tax law requires.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the data I hold about you, or object to how I use it. Email me and I'll respond within a month. You can also complain to the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens.
